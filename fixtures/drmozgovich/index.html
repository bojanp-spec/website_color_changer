Questions & answers

Where do the questions and flashcards come from?
From the documents you upload. The service doesn't write or pick them: AI builds them from your material, and every item points to a page or section and a verbatim quote, so you can check it.
Can I trust the AI?
Not blindly. AI makes mistakes, which is why every item comes with its source, and you can report a wrong card with one click — it disappears from your study.
What about copyright?
You upload the material and you are responsible for having the right to use it for personal study. We don't review documents and we don't publish them; details are in the Terms of Use.
What happens to my file?
The original is deleted within 6 hours, and the extracted text and flashcards after 45 days. You can delete your account and all your data whenever you want.
Which formats can I upload?
PDF, Word (DOC and DOCX) and plain text. If you have a presentation, save it as a PDF and upload that.
How much does it cost?
Your first document is free. After that there are three one-time passes, none of which renews: Monthly ($5.99, 30 days, up to 5 documents), Season ($11.99, 60 days, up to 15 documents and 1,500 pages) and Yearly ($39.99, 365 days, no limit on the number of documents, up to 4,000 pages in total).
